Data Analysis

Real Estate Sales Analysis

Total Actual Sales: Accumulated sales across the entire 20-year period reach roughly $390.06 billion.

Total Assessed Value: The cumulative assessed value (used for tax purposes) sits at approximately $278.37 billion.

Sales Ratio: Defined as {Assessed Value} / \{Sales Amount}. Because assessed values and sales amounts serve entirely different functions, the project correctly notes that they cannot be summed directly as a combined financial aggregate, but rather analyzed independently or as a ratio distribution.

Handling Missing Data (Data Integrity Decision), Justification: The project rationally chooses not to drop these null records because: Many entries correspond to Vacant Land, which naturally does not have a residential or commercial building type assigned. Certain properties are unique structures that do not cleanly fall into standard residential, industrial, or commercial categories, yet possess valid, traceable addresses.
